- [ ] **Rule-6 surgery: stop notifying and calling foreign components while holding locks** *(2026-08-17)* - two dump-proven deadlocks (both captured 2026-08-17 during scenario loads) came from lock-order cycles among `CoreViewModel.m_Lock`, `ProjectScenarioDisplaySettingsViewModel.m_Lock`, `GanttActivitySelectorViewModel.m_Lock` and ReactiveUI's internal `ExpressionChainSink` gates. The point fixes already landed: the lock-free getter sweep (ARCHITECTURE §7 rule 9), hoisting the display-settings core callbacks (`SetIsProjectScenarioUpdated`/`IsReadyToCompile`) out of all the setter lock blocks, and ref-counted revising guards in the Gantt/earned-value selectors (a shared bool let one reviser's `finally` clear the other's guard mid-revision across threads). The REMAINING call-out-under-lock sites are hardening, not live bugs - every remaining edge is one-way with the point fixes in place - and form the deferred surgery, in descending value: (1) `CoreViewModel.ProcessProjectScenario`/`ResetProjectScenario` hold `m_Lock` across the entire load fan-out: raising settings setters, raising `IsReadyToReviseTrackers` (whose `Scheduler.CurrentThread` subscriptions synchronously run selector revisions ON the raising thread, taking selector locks under the core lock - the Core→Selector edge in the first dump), and calling `DisplaySettingsViewModel.SetValues` (the Core→DS edge in the second dump); surgery = split each into a state phase under the lock and a fan-out phase after release, collecting pending raises/signals under the lock and emitting them outside - §7 rule 7 (handlers re-read live state; payloads are stale snapshots) is what makes raise-after-release semantically safe, and the `Begin`/`EndBulkUpdate` window wraps both phases so suppression semantics survive. (2) `ProjectScenarioDisplaySettingsViewModel.SetValues` assigns ~30 raising setters under the DS lock, so every synchronous observer (ReactiveUI sink gates) runs under it; surgery = set fields and collect changed property names under the lock, raise them all after release (this also collapses the setters' reentrant locking). (3) The four selector view models mutate `ObservableCollection`s inside their locked `Set*` methods, so `CollectionChanged` handlers (which raise, and in Gantt/EV write through to DS/Core) execute under the selector lock; surgery = detach the handler around locked mutations and do one manual refresh+raise after release, or compute the change set under the lock and apply it outside. (4) Chart-manager plot-model setters (earned-value/Gantt/resource charts) and `ResourceTrackerSetViewModel.RefreshIndex` raise inside their locks - trivial set-under-lock/raise-after splits. (5) Document the global lock-acquisition DAG in ARCHITECTURE §7 (Manager→Core→DisplaySettings, Core→Selector, node/tracker VMs as leaves; verify core never calls back into the scenario manager under its lock) so future cross-VM calls have an order to conform to.
